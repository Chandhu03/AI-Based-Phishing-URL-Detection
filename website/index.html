SecureMind Labs is an investigation workspace for phishing links. It shows who a link really leads to, which deception techniques it uses, and keeps your cases and indicators together. All of it runs in your browser; the link is never opened or sent.

From one link to a documented case.

The workspace connects four jobs that usually live in separate tools. Each one works today, in your browser, with nothing to install.

Triage reported links

Open a reported email to check its sender, authentication results, links and attachments, or import links from a mail filter or SIEM export. Repeats fold into one alert; each is closed with a disposition or escalated to a case, and the queue shows median time to triage and to close.

Analyse with evidence

Over twenty checks for hidden login text, disguised IPs, look-alike letters, brand misuse and embedded redirects, plus a hostname model. Every finding quotes the part of the link it is about. Paste one link or five hundred.

Investigate

Open a case, follow a response playbook step by step, link analyses, keep notes and track status and priority. Every change is recorded in the case history. Export a Markdown report with every URL defanged.

Track indicators

Record domains, hosts, IPs and URLs with confidence, tags and where they came from. Related indicators are grouped by domain. Import and export plain text, CSV or STIX 2.1.

The link you are worried about never leaves your machine.

Many URL scanners visit the link or forward it to a service. SecureMind reads the text only. The site’s security policy blocks network requests from the workspace, so this is enforced by the browser, not just promised.

  • StoredIn this browser’s IndexedDB. Not synchronised; export a backup to move it.
  • SentNothing. The page loads its own code and rule files, then makes no further requests.
  • Not doneNo DNS, WHOIS or reputation lookups, no screenshots, no link following.
