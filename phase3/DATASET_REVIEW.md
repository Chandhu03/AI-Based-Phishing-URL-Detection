redistribution with attribution. Even so, we do not redistribute either
dataset: `phase3/data/` is gitignored, and the data is fetched with
`python phase3/run.py download`.

## Critical artefact in PhiUSIIL

In PhiUSIIL, **100% of legitimate URLs** are `https://www.<domain>`
homepages with **no path or query**. Phishing URLs differ:

| Property | Share of phishing URLs |
|---|---|
| Scheme is https | 49% |
| Host starts with `www.` | 41% |
| Has a path or query | 28% |

A model that sees the scheme, the `www.` prefix or the path learns this
artefact instead of phishing behaviour. Phase 3 therefore trains on
PhiUSIIL **only with host-level features**, with a leading `www.` removed.
It also measures generalisation on Hannousse, a different dataset collected
in May 2020, where legitimate URLs include paths and both schemes.

## Limitations
- **Unknown sources.** Neither publisher's page states where the URLs came
  from or when PhiUSIIL was collected. Freshness is therefore unknown.
  Hannousse dates from May 2020.
- **No temporal split.** Neither dataset has timestamps.
- **Legitimate URLs are popular domains.** Their homepages under-represent
  long-tail legitimate sites, so false positives on obscure legitimate
  domains are likely under-estimated.
